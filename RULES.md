# Sticky Rules — pi-setup

- **Public repo**: never commit secrets. `models.json`, `mcp.json`, `auth.json`,
  `trust.json`, `advisor-outcomes*`, `sessions/`, `missions/` stay out of
  `config/` — they are blocked by `config/.pi-setup-exclude`; do not weaken it.
- Never publish an artifact built with `--auth` or `--sessions`; the portable
  bundle at repo root is gitignored — only `backups/` is tracked.
- `*.sh` files MUST keep LF line endings (`.gitattributes` enforces it).
- Backup/restore scripts MUST stay non-interactive — no prompts, no `read`,
  no confirmations; risk is handled via snapshots + stderr warnings.
- Language parity: `README.md` (English) and `README.vi.md` (Vietnamese) must
  change together. `docs/pi-setup-migration.md` is Vietnamese-only — keep it
  that way; comments in `.gitignore`/`.pi-setup-exclude` are Vietnamese.
- Never hand-edit `extensions/orca-*.ts`, `extensions/agentkit-*`,
  `extensions/herdr-*.ts` snapshots — they are regenerated by their managers.
